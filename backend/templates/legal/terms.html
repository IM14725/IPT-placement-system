{% blocktrans %} To protect applicants from impulsive decisions and provide fair deliberation time, all submitted applications are subject to a mandatory 24-hour cooling-off period in accordance with the principles of the Electronic Transactions Act, Cap. 442. {% endblocktrans %}

  1. {% trans "Mandatory Review Delay:" %} {% blocktrans %} Companies are restricted from approving or rejecting an application until exactly 24 hours have elapsed from the time of submission. The approval and rejection controls remain disabled within this window. {% endblocktrans %}
  2. {% trans "Exclusive Hold During Window:" %} {% blocktrans %} The slot is temporarily reserved for the applicant during the 24-hour period. Other students cannot apply for the same vacancy until the hold expires or the student cancels. {% endblocktrans %}
  3. {% trans "Student's Right to Withdraw:" %} {% blocktrans %} Students retain the unrestricted right to cancel their application at any point during this 24-hour window, triggering the process described in Section 7. {% endblocktrans %}
  4. {% trans "Post-Window Review:" %} {% blocktrans %} Once the 24-hour period expires, the student's right to independently cancel is suspended. The company is then authorised to issue a final approval or rejection at their discretion. Companies are encouraged to review all pending applications promptly to avoid unnecessary slot unavailability. {% endblocktrans %}
