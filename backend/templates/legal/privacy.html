{% trans "How We Use Your Data" %}

{% trans "3.1 Placement Facilitation" %}

{% blocktrans %} By registering, you consent to your data being shared with prospective employers and educational institutions strictly for placement facilitation purposes. This includes sharing your verified academic profile, uploaded documents, and contact details with companies that you apply to. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "3.2 Your Data Accuracy Obligation" %}

{% blocktrans %} The PDPA 2022 legally mandates that data controllers take reasonable steps to ensure collected personal data is complete, accurate, relevant, and up to date. By accepting these terms, you acknowledge that you bear the primary responsibility to maintain the accuracy of your profile information at all times. {% endblocktrans %}

{% blocktrans %} Students must update their academic information (course, year of study, GPA, and institution) immediately upon any change. Companies must keep their registration details, contact information, and slot listings current and accurate. Profile information provided on the Platform may be relied upon by institutions, companies, and administrators to make binding placement decisions. Providing false or misleading information constitutes a violation of these Terms and the PDPA. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "3.3 Payment Processing" %}

{% blocktrans %} Mobile money transaction data (reference number, amount, mobile number, timestamp) is processed to confirm payment and issue digital receipts. This data is retained for financial audit purposes as required by Tanzanian law. We do not store your full mobile money PIN or bank account credentials. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "3.4 Communications" %}

{% blocktrans %} We use your email and phone number to send system-critical notifications (application updates, payment confirmations, slot acceptance/rejection). Your contact details are not sold to third parties or used for advertising without your explicit consent. {% endblocktrans %}

{% trans "3.5 Security & Fraud Prevention" %}

{% blocktrans %} We process session logs, IP addresses, and audit trails to detect unauthorised access, prevent fraud, and maintain platform integrity. This processing is based on our legitimate interests as Data Controller under the PDPA. If we detect that an account is being used illegally or for purposes outside the intended scope of IPT placement, we may retain and process your data to investigate and prevent further harm. {% endblocktrans %}
